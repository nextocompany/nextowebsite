# Nexto website

The one-page company site at nexto.co.th: a network drawn from the Nexto brain logo travels down the page and tells how Nexto connects scattered clinical data and people.

## Language

### The network story

**Network**:
The Nexto brain drawn as nodes and edges behind the page; the one visual that carries the story from hero to footer.
_Avoid_: animation, background, graph

**Stage**:
A section the network anchors to and takes a shape for (hero, intro, approach, how we work, products, beyond, closing).
_Avoid_: scene, slide, beat

**Beat**:
One step of the network story within or between stages (form, split, start, connect, grow, cluster, shape, reach, close).
_Avoid_: stage, step, phase

**Signal**:
A dot travelling along the network; teal carries data, coral carries people.
_Avoid_: pulse, particle

**Closing**:
The last stage: the network gathered back into the Nexto brain above the closing line, still, with an occasional signal.
_Avoid_: outro, end state

### People

**Engineering partner**:
A company Nexto builds with, named in Beyond. Its marker lights in turn with the others; no line joins it to the network.
_Avoid_: vendor, supplier
