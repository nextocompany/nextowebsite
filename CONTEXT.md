# Nexto website

The one-page company site at nexto.co.th: a network drawn from the Nexto brain logo travels down the page and tells how Nexto connects scattered clinical data and people.

## Language

### The network story

**Network**:
The Nexto brain drawn as nodes and edges behind the page; the one visual that carries the story from hero to footer.
_Avoid_: animation, background, graph

**Stage**:
A section the network anchors to and takes a shape for (hero, intro, approach, how we work, products, beyond, closing).
_Avoid_: scene, slide, beat

**Beat**:
One step of the network story within or between stages (form, split, start, connect, grow, cluster, shape, reach, close).
_Avoid_: stage, step, phase

**Pin**:
A stretch of scroll where the network holds its place on screen and changes beat by beat while the text moves past (Approach and How we work). On wide screens it sits beside the text; on narrow screens it fills the top half.
_Avoid_: sticky, freeze

**Signal**:
A dot travelling along the network; teal carries data, coral carries people.
_Avoid_: pulse, particle

**Closing**:
The last stage: the network gathered back into the Nexto brain above the closing line, still, with an occasional signal.
_Avoid_: outro, end state

### Products

**Endyra**:
Nexto's endoscopy image capture and documentation system, in use in Thailand: Full HD still images by foot switch at an OR kiosk, colonoscopy withdrawal recorded and timed, PDF reports from the clinician portal. Has its own page on nexto.co.th.
_Avoid_: Endyra app, endyra.io

**Anvira**:
Nexto's perioperative anesthesia information system, in development: patient history from the HIS for the pre-anesthetic assessment, an electronic anesthesia record filled from the monitors and anesthesia machine, and an exported record that goes back to the HIS.
_Avoid_: AIMS, anesthesia app

**Manual fallback**:
When Anvira loses its connection to a device, the anesthesiologist records by hand until it reconnects, then automatic recording resumes; the record has no gap.
_Avoid_: offline mode

### People

**Engineering partner**:
A company Nexto builds with, named in Beyond. Its marker lights in turn with the others; no line joins it to the network.
_Avoid_: vendor, supplier

**Partner** (Thai: พันธมิตร):
A company Nexto works alongside, either a medical device company or an engineering partner. In Thai copy it is always พันธมิตร. The medical device partner is Meditop; the site does not look for new ones, so its calls to action speak to hospitals.
_Avoid_: พาร์ทเนอร์, พาร์ตเนอร์, คู่ค้า

**Nexto** (in Thai copy):
The company name stays in Latin letters in every language, including the company line in the footer; on the page the copy says เรา / we once the logo has introduced it.
_Avoid_: เน็กซ์ทู, เน็กซ์โต
